# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to improve shared engineering guidance. We expect everyone who takes part to keep it respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior
- Be courteous. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming otherwise.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Deliberately misleading contributions (invented sources, false verification claims) or content intended to cause harm.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block participants who do not follow these expectations. Report concerns through the repository's private reporting channel or by contacting the maintainers; the maintainers should publish a contact address here before the repository goes public.

This is a short, original statement written for this project. Maintainers who prefer an established community standard may replace it with one after checking its license terms.
